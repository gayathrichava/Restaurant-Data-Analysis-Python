# Restaurant Data Analysis — Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses restaurant data using Python to explore patterns across cuisines, cities, price ranges, online delivery, table booking, restaurant ratings and customer voting activity.

The analysis was completed as part of a **Data Analyst internship at Cognifyz Technologies** and focuses on using exploratory data analysis and visualisation to identify meaningful patterns within restaurant data.

---

## 🎯 Analysis Objectives

The project explores questions such as:

- Which cuisines are most common?
- How do restaurant ratings vary across cities?
- How are restaurants distributed across different price ranges?
- How does online delivery vary across restaurants and price ranges?
- Which restaurant rating categories are most common?
- What are the most common cuisine combinations?
- Which restaurant names appear multiple times in the dataset?
- Is there a relationship between restaurant ratings and customer votes?

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Cuisine Analysis

Analysed restaurant cuisines to identify the most common cuisine categories in the dataset.

### 2. City Analysis

Explored restaurant distribution and average ratings across different cities.

### 3. Price Range Analysis

Examined how restaurants are distributed across different price ranges.

### 4. Online Delivery Analysis

Analysed the availability of online delivery across restaurants.

### 5. Restaurant Rating Analysis

Explored the distribution of restaurant ratings and compared rating patterns across different categories.

### 6. Cuisine Combination Analysis

Identified common combinations of cuisines offered by restaurants.

### 7. Restaurant Chain Analysis

Examined restaurant names appearing multiple times in the dataset to identify restaurant chains.

### 8. Votes and Rating Analysis

Analysed customer voting activity and examined the relationship between the number of votes and restaurant ratings.

### 9. Correlation Analysis

Performed correlation analysis to explore the relationship between restaurant ratings and customer votes.
